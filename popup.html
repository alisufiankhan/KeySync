Privacy & Security

Zero Data Collection

KeySync does not collect, log, track, or transmit any personal data, usage metrics, or 2FA credentials. All data is restricted to your machine.

Local-Only AES-256-GCM

Your secret keys are encrypted on your device using PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds) and AES-256-GCM. Unencrypted secrets are never written to disk.

Network Usage

KeySync runs offline. The only network calls are optional requests to Google Favicons to retrieve icons for custom domains, which are cached locally.
